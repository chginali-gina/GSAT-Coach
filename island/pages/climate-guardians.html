Cut Riverbend’s CO₂ in half by 20502050 年前讓河灣市碳排放減半

Your group is the city’s Climate Committee. Six roles, three years, one city. Test every plan with Activity Data × Emission Factor before you vote.你們這組就是市政府的氣候變遷委員會：六個角色、三個年度、一座城市。每個方案都要先用「活動數據 × 排放係數」算過，再表決。

1. Set up your team1. 建立小組

Only 4 or 5 players? One person can take two roles. Type the same name twice.只有 4–5 人？一人可以兼兩個角色，輸入同一個名字即可。

2. How to play2. 玩法

1Each year you draw 5 policy cards and get 50 coins.每年抽 5 張政策卡，獲得 50 枚金幣。
2Pick a card. All six roles vote. Tie? The Mayor decides.選一張卡，六個角色投票；平手由市長決定。
3Calculate the CO₂ change. Right answer: ★ + 5 coins.算出排放變化，答對得 ★ 與 5 枚金幣。
4After 2050, see your medal and each role’s mission.2050 年結算：看獎章與每個角色的任務。
